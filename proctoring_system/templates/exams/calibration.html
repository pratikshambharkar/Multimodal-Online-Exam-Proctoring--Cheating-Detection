Gaze Calibration

Before {{ exam.title }} begins, RAIM needs a quick, one-time look-around to learn the boundaries of your screen. This makes the "looking away" detector more accurate for your specific camera and seating position, and reduces false alerts for normal head movement.

  • A dot will appear at about nine spots around your screen — corners, edges and center.
  • Simply look directly at the dot while it is shown; no clicking needed.
  • The whole step takes under 30 seconds.

This uses the same webcam permission as the exam itself. If calibration cannot complete, the exam still proceeds with default sensitivity.
