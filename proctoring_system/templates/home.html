What RAIM watches

Face Authentication

DeepFace embeddings verify who is at the keyboard — at login and again at random intervals throughout the exam.

Eye-Gaze Tracking

Head pose and eye-aspect-ratio analysis detects sustained off-screen or downward gaze that suggests notes or a second device.

Object Detection

YOLOv8 spots phones, books and extra people in the camera frame in real time, with confidence-weighted alerts.

Tab-Switch Detection

Browser telemetry flags tab switches, window blur, fullscreen exits, copy and paste — escalating from warnings to termination.

Audio Monitoring

Microphone chunks are screened for speech and conversation so whispered answers do not go unnoticed.

Automated Reports

Every session ends with a trust level, risk timeline and evidence snapshots compiled into an instructor-ready integrity report.

Important notices

  • Webcam and microphone are mandatory for every proctored exam; access must remain granted for the entire attempt.
  • Keep your face visible to the camera at all times — leaving the frame or covering the lens is recorded as a violation.
  • No offline mode is supported — loss of connectivity during an exam is flagged in the integrity report.
  • Exams auto-terminate on repeated violations, including excessive tab switching or persistent identity mismatches.
